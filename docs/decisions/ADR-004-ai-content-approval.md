# ADR-004: Teacher Approval for AI-Generated Curriculum Content

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-10-07

## Context

Model output may be incorrect or unsuitable for a school curriculum.

## Decision

Generated curriculum goes through validation, source grounding, teacher review, and the defined approval process before publication.

## Consequences

Store provenance and approval state. Tutors must retrieve only content permitted by publication/access policy. Contextual tutoring is not itself publication of new curriculum.

## Change policy

Do not silently violate this decision. Propose a superseding ADR if evidence requires a change.
