# ADR-003: Concept-Based Mastery

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-10-07

## Context

Several lessons can teach one concept, and content may change without erasing learning evidence.

## Decision

Attach mastery to student/concept/tenant, not simply a lesson. Keep evidence/history and versioned algorithms.

## Consequences

XP and completion are distinct from mastery. Start with simple deterministic estimation and revision rules.

## Change policy

Do not silently violate this decision. Propose a superseding ADR if evidence requires a change.
